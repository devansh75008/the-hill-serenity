Our Story & Sustainable Soul

The Hill Serenity was born from a simple reverence: that the true luxury of the mountains is not gold and glass, but clean pine air, sacred silence, unhurried hospitality, and harmony with the earth.

Every wall of our sanctuary was raised by local Kumaoni masons using native Chafi river stones and slate shingles that naturally insulate against cold mountain winters and keep interiors pleasantly cool throughout summer. We preserve centuries-old timber traditions while adopting zero-waste, eco-conscious stewardship.

🌱 Zero Single-Use Plastic

Natural mountain spring water treated on-site and served in sanitized glass carafes. Biodegradable bamboo and brass bath amenities.

🤝 Local Community First

Over 80% of our team members are residents of Chafi and adjoining villages, trained in world-class boutique hospitality standards.

💧 Rainwater & Solar Energy

Integrated rainwater harvesting replenishes our fruit orchard, while roof-mounted solar thermal systems supply continuous clean hot water.

🍎 Organic Estate Orchards

Apples, plums, walnuts, and wild mint harvested right on our grounds, supplying our daily kitchen and preserves.

Spiritual Pilgrimage Manual

Visiting Kainchi Dham: The Complete Devotee Guide

Established in the 1960s by the revered saint Neem Karoli Baba (affectionately known as Maharaj-ji), Kainchi Dham is situated in a lush pine valley alongside the Kshipra river. It is a powerful centre of unconditional love, divine presence, and spiritual quietude.

1. Temple Timings & Daily Routine

The Ashram gates generally open at 6:00 AM in Summer and 7:00 AM in Winter. Morning Aarti is performed shortly after opening. The evening Aarti takes place at sunset (approx. 6:30 PM in Summer and 5:30 PM in Winter), preceded by the chanting of Sri Hanuman Chalisa and bhajans.

2. How Staying in Chafi Gives You the Advantage

Visitors staying in Nainital or Bhowali often face severe traffic jams along the narrow mountain highway, particularly on weekends and the 15th June Bhandara day. Staying at The Hill Serenity in Chafi (12 km away) gives you a quiet, nature-immersed haven and allows you to arrive at the temple doors before tour buses and day-trippers arrive.

3. Temple Etiquette & Offerings

  • Dress Code: Modest, respectful traditional Indian attire or quiet clothing covering shoulders and knees.
  • Prasad: Plain roasted chickpeas (chana), jaggery, or seasonal fruit are customary simple offerings.
  • Blanket Offering: Devotees frequently bring warm blankets in tribute to Baba's simple attire.
  • Photography: Photography and mobile video recording are strictly prohibited within inner sanctums.
